Process

From application to decision
in under 3 seconds

trbo reads real financial documents and generates a cross-border credit score using our 7-factor scoring engine.

1
Borrower applies
Via your branded portal link. Uploads bank statement, payslip, remittance proof.
2
trbo reads documents
AI extracts income, savings, employer, and transfer history from real files.
3
Score is generated
300–850 global score across 7 weighted factors. APR and credit limit suggested.
4
Lender decides
Auto-approve, auto-decline, or route to manual review based on your thresholds.
Features

Built for the full lending lifecycle

Real Document Parsing
Upload any PDF bank statement, payslip, or remittance receipt. trbo extracts real numbers — no forms to fill.
7-Factor Credit Score
Income stability, savings rate, remittance consistency, employment tenure, document completeness, cash flow health.
Affiliate Links
Generate branded borrower links per campaign. Track clicks, conversions, and corridor performance in real time.
White-Label Portal
Borrowers see your brand, your domain, your colors. trbo is invisible infrastructure underneath.
Pricing Engine
Set score bands, APR ranges, credit limits, DTI rules, and corridor restrictions per lender account.
Webhooks & API
Real-time events fired to your endpoint on every decision. Full REST API for deep integration.
