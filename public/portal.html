Process

From application to decision
in under 3 seconds

trbo reads real financial documents and generates a cross-border credit score using our 7-factor scoring engine.

1
Borrower applies
Via your branded portal link. Uploads bank statement, payslip, remittance proof.
2
trbo reads documents
AI extracts income, savings, employer, and transfer history from real files.
3
Score is generated
300–850 global score across 7 weighted factors. APR and credit limit suggested.

Auto-approve, auto-decline, or route to manual review based on your thresholds.
